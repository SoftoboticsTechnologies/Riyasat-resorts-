Where heritage meets hospitality

A 3 star resort in Indirapuram, Ghaziabad, designed in a heritage look and run by a team that has been in hospitality and events for six years.

A property built around the event

Well appointed rooms with modern amenities, a beautiful garden and a swimming pool sit on one property with a heritage face. The focus, from the beginning, has been events.

Our philosophy is simple: experimenting with spaces and atmosphere gives better results. Every event is shaped around what the client envisions, which is why no two celebrations here look the same.
